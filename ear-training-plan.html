0–7Just Starting

Guessing gets you about 6 or 7, so this isn't a bad score, it just means your ear hasn't been trained yet.

  1. Play one note, and sing it back.
  2. Then play two notes, and say higher or lower.
  3. Hum the first line of Happy Birthday or Twinkle Twinkle before you play it.
8–11Waking Up
  1. Play the C major scale, and sing the numbers as you go.
  2. Then play a random note, and name its number.
  3. Hum a note before you play it, then check if you were right.
12–15Ready to Play
  1. Pick an easy, well-known song, like Let It Be or a song from your own set.
  2. Find the bass note of every chord in it.
  3. Once a week, learn one new song by ear only, no chords looked up.
